## Release rule

- Keep existing claims frozen until evidence is attached and reviewed.
- If evidence cannot be supplied, prefer neutral wording such as “Selected work”
  over endorsement language such as “Trusted by”; do not retain unsupported
  role, award, or credit wording for visual impact.
- Record the source, verification date, permitted public wording, and asset-use
  permission before changing `index.html`.
- Production remains unchanged until the relevant Draft PR stack is merged and
  the custom domain passes a real smoke test.

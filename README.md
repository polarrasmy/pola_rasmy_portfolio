# rasmy.co

The RASMY company site: film production between Cairo and China, led by Pola Rasmy.
Live at https://rasmy.co (GitHub Pages, deployed from `main`).

## What is here
- `index.html` — the whole site: one page, inline CSS and JS, no build step.
- `404.html`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `CNAME` — hosting and SEO files.
- `*.webp` — the images the page uses. `fonts/inter-latin.woff2` — the only font.
- `RASMY-Rate-Card*.pdf` — rate cards in English, Arabic and Chinese, linked from the Packages section.
- `Pola-Rasmy-QR.png` — QR code used outside the site; keep it.

## Changing the site
Edit `index.html`, open it in a browser to check, then merge to `main`. Pages publishes within a minute.
Prices live in two places that must match: the Packages section in `index.html` and the rate card PDFs
(their sources are in the `Pola` repo under `sales/`).

## Older versions
Earlier RASMY sites were separate repos. Their full history is kept here as branches:
- `archive/rasmy-films` — "Cinematic Car & Product Films" landing page (June 2026)
- `archive/rasmy-studio`, `archive/rasmy-studio--fix-studio-conversion` — "Cinematic AI Ad Films" landing page (June–July 2026)

Nothing from them was carried into the current site: their positioning and credits were replaced by the
audited claims on the current page.
